A few useful answers.

Have another question? Contact Green Gate EPC.

What does an EPC tell me?

An Energy Performance Certificate shows a property's energy efficiency rating on an A to G scale and includes recommendations for improving its performance. The rating card on this website is an illustration.

What information should I provide for a quote?

The postcode, property type, number of bedrooms and reason for the assessment are a useful start. Include preferred dates and any access arrangements. For several properties, provide each address in your message.

How should I prepare for the visit?

Arrange access to the property and tell us about any restrictions. If you have documents relating to insulation, heating or other improvements, mention them when booking so we can advise what is useful.

How much will it cost and when can you visit?

Contact us for a property-specific quote and current availability. Any price, appointment and completion timescale will be agreed directly with you.

Does the quote form send my details?

Yes. When you submit the form, FormSubmit processes your details and forwards the enquiry to Green Gate EPC by email.
